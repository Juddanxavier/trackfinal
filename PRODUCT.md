# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers who have shipped parcels through Gajan Traders. Their primary job is entering a tracking or whitelabel ID to see where their package is and when it will arrive. Secondary users are logistics staff who may look up tracking for customer support.

## Product Purpose

A shipment tracking tool that takes a tracking code or whitelabel ID and returns real-time delivery status with a timeline of events. It exists to give customers visibility into their shipments without calling support. Success is a customer who can answer "where is my package?" from the page alone.

## Brand Personality

Fast, Trusted, Global. Red accent communicates urgency and action. Voice is direct and informative — no marketing gloss, just clear status information.

## Anti-references

- Over-designed logistics dashboards with extraneous visual fluff
- AI-generated corporate templates with gradient text and glassmorphism
- Cluttered tracking pages that bury the status information
- Generic courier sites with stock photography and no personality

## Design Principles

1. **Status first.** The shipment status and timeline are the reason the page exists. Place them above everything else, make them scannable at a glance.
2. **Trust through clarity.** Every element should answer a question the customer actually has. No decorative filler, no redundant copy.
3. **One job, well done.** The tracking page does one thing. Don't add distractions, cross-sells, or upsells.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Tracking info must be readable by anyone regardless of device or ability. Timeline events must be understandable without relying on color alone.
